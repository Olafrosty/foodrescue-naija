How We Align With SDGs

🥘

SDG 2: Zero Hunger

We ensure surplus food from restaurants, supermarkets and events reaches NGOs, shelters and low-income families in Rivers, Lagos, Oyo and beyond — 100% FREE, no profit.

♻️

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption & Production

Nigeria wastes 40% of food. Our platform reduces waste by creating a real-time marketplace for surplus before expiry.

🌍

SDG 13: Climate Action

Food waste in landfill = methane. Every kg saved = CO₂ saved. Dashboard tracks environmental impact live.
