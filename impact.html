SECURITY FIRST: How we prevent kidnapping & scams in Ibadan

HUB-ONLY • NO HOME ADDRESS
1. No Home Pickups - BANNED

All food is dropped at Dugbe Hub or partnered churches/mosques/markets. No private house address is ever shown on the app. If user tries to type house, system blocks it.

2. Phone Verification

Volunteer calls donor before listing goes live to verify food and identity. No verification, no listing. Scammers are filtered out.

3. Daylight Only (8AM - 6PM)

Pickup time is locked to daylight hours. System rejects night pickups. All pickups happen in crowded public places.

4. Pickup Code: No code, no food

4-digit code auto-generated. Receiver must show code at Hub. Prevents impersonation and ensures only verified collector gets food.

5. Never Alone Rule

App forces safety modal before claiming: "Never go alone, share live location with family" + WhatsApp share location button.

6. Report & Ban - 2 Reports = Hidden

Any suspicious listing can be reported. After 2 reports, food auto-hides as "Under Review". Repeat offenders phone number blocked. Emergency: 199 / 112

7. NGO Priority - Food goes to verified NGOs first

Food goes to verified NGOs, orphanages, mosques, churches first — not random individuals. We partner with Ibadan Food Bank, UI campus groups, Bodija market women associations. This reduces risk of one-on-one strangers meeting.

How We Align With SDGs

SDG 2: Zero Hunger — Ibadan First

We prioritize Oyo State: surplus from restaurants in Dugbe, bakeries in Bodija, events at UI reaches NGOs, orphanages & low-income families in Ogbomoso & Challenge — 100% free.

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption

Nigeria wastes 40% of food. Our Ibadan marketplace rescues food before expiry — real-time alerts for pickup in under 2 hours.

🌍

SDG 13: Climate Action

Landfill in Ajakanga, Ibadan emits methane. Every kg we save = 2.5kg CO₂ avoided. Dashboard shows live carbon savings for Oyo State.

SDG 16: Safety & Trust

Security is part of sustainability. By banning home addresses, enforcing Hub-only daylight pickup, phone verification and pickup codes, we prevent kidnapping, scams and ritual risks that destroy community trust. Safe food rescue is sustainable food rescue.
